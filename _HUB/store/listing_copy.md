## 4. Description

### EN-US

> **The WakeApp is your personal control center for focus and discipline.**
>
> Most focus apps box you into someone else's routine. The WakeApp does the opposite: you build the setup that fits your life, then run it without friction. Two ideas drive everything — make it yours, and keep it simple.
>
> **Block what distracts you**
> Choose the apps and websites that pull you away, and restrict them on your terms. Start a focus session and they're out of reach until you're done.
>
> **Focus sessions, your way**
> - Focus Mode — a timed session that keeps distractions blocked
> - Blackout Mode — a stricter session for deep work
> - Classic Blocking — keep selected apps and sites restricted all day
>
> **Plan your day around real limits**
> - Planned Pauses — schedule restriction windows automatically
> - Daily Limits — cap time on the apps that eat your day
>
> **Built around you**
> Customize your sessions, schedules, goals and habits into one control center — then keep the daily experience clean and simple.
>
> **Private by design**
> On iPhone, blocking uses Apple's official Screen Time (Family Controls) frameworks. The apps and websites you pick stay on your device — they're never uploaded or shared.
>
> **WakeApp Premium**
> Unlock unlimited focus sessions, blocking rules, schedules and daily limits. Auto-renewable subscription billed through the App Store; cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.
>
> Take back your screen time. Build your control center.
